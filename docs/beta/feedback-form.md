# End-of-beta feedback form

Create a free **Google Form** (forms.google.com) with these questions, then share its link with testers in the second week.
Keep it to about 2 minutes, so most people finish it.

1. **How many times did you post a photo?** (multiple choice: 0 / 1 / 2–5 / more than 5)
2. **Would you use Traveliary on your next trip?** (linear scale 1–5: *Definitely not* → *Definitely*)
3. **What was confusing or didn't work?** (paragraph)
4. **Would you want a profile that keeps your photos if you change phones?** (multiple choice: *Yes, I'd sign in for that* / *Nice to have* / *No, I like it without accounts*)
5. **What's the one thing that would make you use it more?** (paragraph)

Optional, at the end: **Phone:** iPhone / Android, and **email for updates** (short answer, not required).

## What the answers decide
- **Q2 mostly 4–5 and Q1 > 1:** the core idea works. Go to the store phase.
- **Q4 mostly "Yes":** add optional sign-in (Sign in with Apple / email) before or right after launch.
- **Q3 and Q5:** the fix list. Fix crashes and blockers before launch, and put the rest on the backlog.
