# Traveliary beta: tester guide

Thanks for testing **Traveliary**! It's a travel diary in photos: take a photo where you are, and see who else has been there.
The beta runs for about **2 weeks**. Please use it on your trips and walks, and tell us what you think.

## Install

### Android
1. Open this link on your phone: (Android link sent to you privately) (about 145 MB, so use Wi-Fi)
2. Download the file and tap it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser, then tap **Install**.

### iPhone (via the free Expo Go app)
1. Create a free account at **expo.dev** and send your **Expo username** to Jonnel, who will invite you.
2. Accept the invite email.
3. Install **Expo Go** from the App Store and **log in** with that account.
4. Open this link on your iPhone (tap it in Messages or Notes): (iPhone link sent to you privately)
5. Next time, open Expo Go → Traveliary is under **Recently opened**.

## Please try
- **Post a photo** where you are: Camera tab → shutter → **Post publicly**.
- **Camera tools:** pinch to zoom, **0.5×** (on iPhones with an ultra-wide lens), flash, and the grid.
- **Edit before posting:** on the preview tap **Edit** → crop, rotate, filters and brightness/contrast/saturation/warmth.
- **Explore** the map and the **Nearby** list. Open places and photos.
- **Must see** (Explore → Must see): famous places within 50 km. Open one for directions and its story. The first time in a new area it can take up to 30 seconds.
- **Allow notifications**, so you hear when someone posts at a place you've been.
- **Post without signal** once, e.g. in Airplane mode. It should upload by itself later.
- Visit a place another tester posted from, and post there too.

## Good to know
- **Your posts are public.** Others see the place name, never your exact position.
- **Don't delete or reinstall the app (or Expo Go)** during the beta. There are no accounts yet, so you'd lose access to your posts.
- **See something inappropriate?** Open the photo → **Report photo**, or **Block traveler**.
- **Delete everything:** My Trips → ⓘ → **Delete my photos and identity**.

## Feedback
- **Quick:** My Trips → ⓘ → **Send feedback** (opens an email with your app version).
- **End of beta:** a short form with 5 questions, link to follow.
- **Bugs:** a screenshot plus what you did just before helps a lot.
